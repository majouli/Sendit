<h1 align="center">Sendit</h1>

<p align="center"><em>That's what we do.</em></p>

**Tired of twenty addons that all look different?** Sendit is one suite with
one look and one window. Chat, gear ratings, quests, dungeons, dungeon maps, talents,
graphics, transmog - it all shares the same design, the same language and the same
profiles. Pick a palette once and everything follows.

- **Looks designed, not bolted on:** three palettes (Dusk, Light, Wood) and two
  frame styles - clean modern lines or the game's own gold borders.
- **Speaks your language:** English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Русский.
  Switch any time, no reload.
- **Made for the Forever client:** Blizzard's frames stay untouched underneath,
  so nothing breaks protected actions.
- **Take what you like:** every module can be switched off on its own.
- **Apps at your fingertips:** a little app bar slides out Messenger, Upgrade,
  Quests, Dungeons, Maps, Wardrobe and the Dribbler.

## What's inside

| Module | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Sendit** (core) | The Sendit window with design, language, profiles, the minimap button and the app bar. Every module needs it. |
| **Fade** | Your UI disappears when nothing's going on and comes right back for combat, targets, casting, the mouse, typing, group chat or loot. Works with Blizzard's frames and other addons' alike. |
| **Chat** | A chat that finally feels calm: movable windows, tabs you can pull out, your own tabs for channels and message types, a combat log with filters in the same look, and all whispers in one messenger with your friends list - pin the chats that matter to the top. Loot and rolls get summed up in a single line, and **live rolls** ✨ show above the chat who picks what and reveal the numbers one by one, lead changes included - `/roll` too. |
| **GearScore** | GearScore on items, on you and on anyone you hover or inspect - plus their talents. |
| **Harvest** | See what disenchanting, skinning, mining and herbalism give you, with auction prices, right in the tooltip. The **Dribbler** turns disenchanting into a gold run: buy the most profitable items at the auction house, disenchant each with one click and watch your earnings. |
| **Upgrade** | How much better is that item, really? Upgrade recalculates your whole character - damage, healing or survival - for every class and talent tree. Alt-click any item to compare. ✨ **Upgrade scan:** every item in the auction house rated for you, the best upgrades per slot, bought in two clicks. ✨ **Green arrows** mark every upgrade in your bags, loot, rolls, at the merchant and on quest rewards. |
| **Quests** | Over 6,000 Forever quests, offline. Search texts, items, NPCs or IDs (wildcards too), filter by zone, dungeon or raid and faction, follow whole quest chains with rewards, and right-click any quest for a map pin on its giver, objective or turn-in. |
| **Dungeons** | Every dungeon, its bosses and their loot - and how much each drop would improve your gear. Bosses without an encounter (Aku'mai, Old Serra'kis, The Baron and more) and what drops anywhere in an instance are in too. |
| **Maps** 🗺️ new | A map of every dungeon and raid, level by level - drawn from the instance's real rooms, or Blizzard's original maps. **Inside the instance you see yourself on it** 📍: an arrow that walks and turns with you, the map follows your level. Press **M** in an instance to open it, or turn on the **compact map** that follows you in a corner. Bosses sit on the map with their portrait: click one for its loot and how much it improves your gear; ❌ bosses you killed in this run are crossed out. |
| **Talents** | Talent routes for every tree, right in Blizzard's talent window: leveling, dungeons & raids and PvP builds as cards that say what each build is for and what its talents do. The next talent glows, free points are numbered, and a timeline shows all 51 points. Go your own way and the route adapts. |
| **Graphics** | Squeeze the last bit of beauty out of the game: denser grass, longer view distance, more detail in the distance, sharper shadows - everything the graphics menu can't reach, as simple sliders. A "!" warns you where it starts costing frame rate, tooltips explain every setting, and raids can get their own values. One click on **Maximum** and you're there. |
| **Experience** | Every bit of experience you earn - kills, quests, exploring, level-ups - tumbles down as little glowing bubbles that fly into your character, and the gain rises above your head. |
| **Sell** | At a merchant, every bag item sorted by where it's worth most: the merchant, the auction house or disenchanting. Upgrades - also for your second spec - and items you mark stay in your bags; tick what should go and sell it all with one click. |
| **Wardrobe** | Every transmog look on a 3D model of your character: full sets, highlights like legendaries and cosmetics, all single looks - and your own outfits, piece by piece. Every piece says where it comes from: auction price, quest, dungeon boss, vendor or drop with a map pin, crafting, PvP or reputation. One click opens the quest or the boss. |
| **Macros** | Ready macros for every class and specialization - mouseover heals and curses, interrupts that stop your own cast, stance and form switches, openers, cooldown combos. Spell names come from your client, so they work in every language. Drag one onto your bar. |

## Getting started

- `/sendit` or the minimap button opens the window - every module has its own
  pages there.
- **App bar:** click the round Sendit handle to slide out the module windows,
  drag it wherever you like, right-click for the Sendit window. Don't want it?
  Turn it off under Design.
- **Live rolls:** `/chat rolltest` plays a loot roll and a `/roll` duel so you
  can see it without a group. Middle-click hides a box above the chat.
- **Graphics:** `/sendit graphics`. Pick a group at the top, drag the sliders,
  done. Your values stick, even when you change the preset in the graphics menu.
- **Experience:** on its page, click **Play** and move the height slider until
  the bubbles fly right into your character.
- **Dribbler:** open the auction house, click the disenchant tab on the right,
  start and buy from the list.
- **Upgrade scan:** `/gc ah` or the arrow button beside the auction house. Tick
  slots, armor and weapon types on the left - the list follows at once. The light
  top right tells how old the auction data is.
- **Auction data away from the auction house:** download `SenditAuctions.exe`
  from the release, start it, enter a free Battle.net API client
  ([create one here](https://develop.battle.net/access/clients)) and click
  **Set up**. It fetches your auction house every 5 minutes in the background;
  in game `/reload` loads it. The same window updates and uninstalls it.
- **Sell:** talk to a merchant and the list opens beside it. Click a tile to tick
  it, right-click to always keep it, then **Sell**.
- **Wardrobe:** `/wardrobe` or the app bar. Click a set or look to put it on, drag the model to turn it, the wheel zooms. Click a source on the right for its map pin, quest or boss; name your outfit and **Save** it. Gear seen in the auction house that the wardrobe doesn't know yet is added by itself.
- **Maps:** inside a dungeon or raid press **M** (the world map is one click away
  in the window). Wait a moment for your arrow to appear; the map follows your
  level. Click a boss for its loot, turn on the compact map with the button at
  the top. Settings - Maps picks the map style (Drawn, Blizzard, Rooms).
- **Talents:** open the talent window and click the route button bottom left:
  one column per tree, one card per build - hover a card to see what it gives
  you. Fold out the timeline with the tab on the right.
- **Profiles** hold your design and the settings of every module. Pick one and
  it's active right away - all characters on the account can share it.

## Commands

| Command | |
|---|---|
| `/sendit` | the Sendit window (`/sendit profile <name>`, `/sendit <page>`) |
| `/sendit graphics` | the graphics sliders |
| `/fade`, `/fa` | Fade |
| `/chat` | the messenger (`/chat rolltest`, `/chat reset`, `/chat off`, `/chat on`) |
| `/gs`, `/gearscore` | GearScore (`/gs score`, `/gs auto`) |
| `/harvest`, `/dei` | Harvest |
| `/upgrade`, `/gc` | the compare window (`/gc spec ret\|holy\|prot\|auto`), `/gc ah` the upgrade scan |
| `/sell` | the sell list (at a merchant) |
| `/sq` | Quests |
| `/sd` | Dungeons |
| `/wardrobe`, `/swr` | Wardrobe |
| `/maps` | Maps (inside an instance also **M**) |

## Installation

**WowUp:** install straight from this repository's latest release.
**By hand:** unpack the release zip into
`World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\` (one folder per module) and
restart the game.
